Executive summary

A commoditized category with four clear openings

The niche splits into enterprise generalists, affordable specialists, and the field-service CRMs' own built-in tools. The features are commoditized — the wins are price, focus, platform breadth, and compliance.

The opening

Nobody owns "home-services-only, affordable, contract-free, and compliance-clean."

Job-triggered SMS+email requests, CRM integrations, AI replies, widgets and social posting are now table stakes (GatherUp ships them all from $99/mo). Birdeye sells the enterprise at $299–$449/mo per location on annual contracts; NiceJob owns the affordable tier but leans on a negative-feedback funnel that risks Google/FTC review-gating bans; the CRMs' built-in tools only post to Google + Facebook.

TableTurnerr wins by being home-services-only, flat and month-to-month, multi-platform (Google + Facebook + Yelp + Angi), and compliance-clean — and by running the niche's proven GTM (free grader + "alternative" pages + CRM marketplaces).

Compliance & risk

The rules that bite

Review gating is banned by Google and the FTC; SMS is governed by TCPA. This shapes both product and copy.

Don't
  • No review gating — routing unhappy customers to a private form is banned (FTC Final Rule, Oct 2024; Google removed 292M+ violating reviews in 2025). NiceJob-style funnels flirt with this.
  • No paying for / incentivizing reviews.
  • No fake reviews or testimonials (FTC).
Do
  • SMS opt-out (TCPA, Apr 11 2025): honor STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE within 10 business days; covers calls + texts.
  • Register A2P 10DLC + automatic STOP handling.
  • Ask every customer and let reviews land publicly.
